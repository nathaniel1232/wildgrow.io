## 3. How it works (3 steps)

**01 · Frame your app.** Two minutes, no account needed. Wildfire derives your positioning,
your content pillars, and the exact viewer you're trying to reach — because relevance, not
reach, is what the algorithm rewards.

**02 · Get ideas built for the one metric that matters.** Short-form is ranked mostly on
**watch-time and completion**, with **shares** as the strongest signal. Every idea Wildfire
generates is engineered for that: a hook for the first two seconds, a tight loop, and a
reason to send it to a friend — hook → beats → on-screen text → caption → hashtags → sound.

**03 · Run the plan and compound.** Warm up the account, launch, and keep posting on a
30/60/90 schedule. Each video is a fresh, free shot in front of a new audience. The ones
that land teach the engine what to make next.

**Idea Engine.** Fully-scripted video ideas tuned to your app — not generic prompts. Hook,
beat-by-beat structure, on-screen text, caption, hashtags, and a suggested sound, with a

and compound. Sequencing is how Robinhood turned a waitlist into **a million signups before
it even launched** — each step fed the next.
([Prefinery](https://www.prefinery.com/blog/referral-programs/prelaunch-campaign/robinhood/))

**30 / 60 / 90 Plan.** A roadmap from a cold account to your first breakout. Volume and

by posting relentlessly and mining its comments for the next idea.
([Startup Spells](https://startupspells.com/p/duolingo-tiktok-playbook-850m-views-143-viral-videos))

**Viral Academy.** Short lessons on how the For You page actually decides what to show —

## 6. Objections / FAQ

**"Isn't 'going viral' just luck?"**
Mostly it's not luck — and mostly it's not a magic self-spreading loop either. Research on
how content actually spreads found that **over 90% of messages don't spread at all**, and
about **95% of what people see comes straight from the original source** — meaning growth
comes from *engineered* moments (a strong post the algorithm pushes, a launch, a creator),
not happy accidents. Wildfire is the system for taking that shot, over and over.
([Lenny Rachitsky](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/virality-is-a-myth-mostly))

**"I have zero followers. Doesn't that doom me?"**
No. TikTok states outright that **follower count is not a ranking factor** — every video
gets tested on a fresh audience and rises or falls on watch-time. That's the whole reason a
founder with no following can break through.
([TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you))

**"Will one viral video make my app?"**
Probably not on its own — and we won't pretend otherwise. Even a #2 Product Hunt finish only
sent Plausible about **36 trials**. What works is *compounding*: many shots, an audience you
own, and a product people keep. Wildfire is built around the 30/60/90 compound, not a single
lottery ticket. ([Plausible](https://plausible.io/blog/product-hunt-launch))

**"Does Wildfire have testimonials / how many users do you have?"**
We're new and we're honest about it. Rather than invent reviews or user counts, we show real,
sourced results from the companies whose playbook this is built on — and we're building in
public, so our own numbers will show up here as they're real. The indie community now
actively distrusts fake metrics (it's why tools like TrustMRR exist), and so do we.
([Startup Series](https://startupseries.io/how-indie-hacker-marc-lou-monetised-mrr-bragging/))

**"Do I need to pay for ads or creators?"**
No. Short-form organic reach is free — that's the point. Paid creator seeding (the model
Cal AI used: ~150 creators on retainer) can accelerate things later, but it's optional and
we're clear that it's *paid* distribution, not organic. Wildfire starts you on the free
channel. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/16/photo-calorie-app-cal-ai-downloaded-over-a-million-times-was-built-by-two-teenagers/))

**"Do I need an API key?"**
No. Wildfire ships with a high-quality built-in engine, so everything works out of the box.
Add your own model key any time for higher-quality generation.
